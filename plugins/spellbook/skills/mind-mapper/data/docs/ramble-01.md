# Ramble — morning walk, thinking about Hollowbrook

Okay so. Thinking about the magic economy again. Every time small magic shows up
in the drafts, somebody ends up owing somebody, and I keep noticing that nobody
ever settles a debt in coin. It's always loaves. I think the pattern is real:
small magic is settled in baked goods, and I don't know yet if that's a rule of
the world or just Maren's gravity pulling every scene toward the bakery. Worth
watching before we write it down as law.

Also the brook. Two stories now where someone remarks the brook runs quiet and
nobody follows it up. If the brook is quiet because something is listening,
that's a thread. If it's quiet because the Rules say running water keeps
secrets, that's cosmology and it should stay off the page like the rest of it.

And I keep circling the ferryman line from Story 3. There's no ferry in
Hollowbrook. There's no river you'd ferry across. So either the ferryman works
the brook — which makes the quiet worse, in a good way — or he works something
that isn't water at all. I want him on the board but I'm not ready to make him
real.
