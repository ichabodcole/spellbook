# Hollowbrook

The village itself: a brook that runs quieter than it should, houses that lean
toward each other, and a habit of not asking why.

The brook runs through the village center and past the mill. Its quiet is
remarked on by villagers and never explained — currently an open thread, not
canon (see Threads).

Small magic is ambient here: warmed doorknobs, bread that never stales inside
the bounds, lamps that light a half-breath before Old Wick reaches them. The
village treats all of it as manners, not wonder.

Geography anchors: the bakery at center, the millpond east by the mill, the
crossing-stones at the north path where the Edge begins.
