# Maren

The village baker of Hollowbrook. Steady hands, unsteady patience. Her small
magic lives in proofing dough and reading weather in flour dust — she would call
it craft, not magic, and correct you once.

Keeps the bakery at the village center. Half the stories start there because
bread is where news travels.

Her rivalry with Edda is old, ritualized, and load-bearing: they contest every
festival bake, and neither would survive its ending. Treat the rivalry as
furniture, not plot — it moves only if a story earns it.

Apprentice: Tam. She took him on after the flood year and does not explain why.
She watches his fascination with the Edge the way you watch a pot you know will
boil over.
