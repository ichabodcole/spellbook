# The Edge

The forest at the village bound. Deep, non-personal, older than the village's
memory of itself. It is not hostile; it is indifferent, which is worse.

Register rule: the Edge is never cozy. It does not shelter, comfort, or reward.
Scenes set at or near the Edge drop the village's warm domestic register
entirely — this is the strongest tonal boundary in the world and the one most at
risk of drift.

The Edge bounds Hollowbrook on the north and east; the crossing-stones mark
where the village's small courtesies stop working. What the Edge does with magic
is cosmology (see Rules of Small Magic) and stays off the page.
