# The cutover

## Context

This was the sixth and last Workspace Kit cycle, the second half of the split
the shell cycle's survey made ([cycle](../../../cycles/2026-10-cutover.md),
[shell cycle](../../../cycles/2026-10-shell.md)). A refreshed survey on
2026-10-20 found the flip small but coupled to acc and the CLI golden. It also
found that every one of Cole's sessions converted on current code. Cole approved
the cycle, its three new items and four from triage, and ruled that his trial
would run on his real `~/.scriptorium`. The branch was `cycle/cutover`. It
landed on `develop` twice: the flip and the SKILL on 2026-10-20, so Cole could
try the build, and the deletion on 2026-10-22 after his go.

## What happened

- **Wave 1:** four implementers in parallel. They built the flip (one commit
  carrying the hatch, the census swap and the golden), the split divider, the
  closed-composer announcement and the timeout refusal. Each had a no-stake
  verifier. Three went back for missing tests the verifiers' planted regressions
  exposed; one of those (the divider's default size) turned out to be a real
  bug.
- **Wave 2:** the SKILL rewrite and the census staleness ward. The SKILL took
  three verify passes. The first found claims the build contradicted, and a bare
  `tail` that never ended after the human ended the session. The second found
  that the first fix regressed: a tail armed beside `open` stopped on the old
  session. The third passed the grace-window fix at 28 of 28 timings.
- **The trial (2026-10-22).** Cole used the flipped build in a real session. It
  filed five items and found no regression. One of them, the End session dialog
  overflowing, joined the cycle.
- **Wave 3:** the deletion. The verifier ran the CLI's full surface on both
  sides with identical output, pixel-diffed the page, and drove a copied real
  session end to end.

## Decisions

- The trial runs on the real home; old session data is not a concern (Cole).
- The hatch is refused once any session is in the kit's shape.
- A bare `tail` stops on a human or timeout end only after `open`'s default
  start window, and at once on an agent end. Not taken: a "starting" marker
  written before the spawn, which would remove the window. It is a later option
  if the 45 s wait bites.
- `backend/kit/` keeps its name. Renaming it is churn that two wards and about
  40 test paths pin.

## Review

Every item had a no-stake verifier that ran its claims. Several items had more
than one. Each verifier planted regressions, and the survivors became tests. The
final gate on `cycle/cutover` had 6,084 pass and 0 fail. The count dropped from
about 7,100 because the shipped tests went with the shipped code.
