# WorkspaceShell's held selection and reveal (W3) — 2026-11-25

## Context

The third step in lane B, run beside S4. `WorkspaceShell.tsx` was 1,666 lines
(Biome's count 1,498). The held selection and the reveal move together because a
comment orders their effects: the pending show is declared after the effect that
clears a reveal when the document changes.

## What Happened

- **Two hooks, one commit.** `useHeldSelection.ts` (185 lines) and
  `useReveal.ts` (125), called one after the other where the sections sat, each
  body byte-identical to its section. On develop no `ShellBody` effect sat
  between or inside the two sections, so every effect keeps its place in the
  component's order.
- **What they take and give.** `useHeldSelection({ daemon, state, send })`
  returns the 23 names `ShellBody` still uses.
  `useReveal({ daemon, toasts, send, openDoc, requestReveal })` returns
  `setPendingShow`, `openAt` and `openResult`. `openDoc` and `requestReveal`
  pass from one to the other through `ShellBody`, so swapping the two calls
  would not compile.
- **The selection's range** includes the refs the next message draws on
  (`treeSelected`, `addedRefs`, `addRef`, `removeRef`, `clearRefs`), as the
  write-up's range does: leaving them would have meant reordering hooks.
- **Two dependencies added** at Biome's request, `setPendingShow` and
  `setTreeSelected`, both raw `useState` setters, so no effect runs more often.
- **The override** is 1,293 (was 1,498), the exact count. No test changed. The
  gate's build rebuilt the scriptorium bundle (`chore(dist)`).
