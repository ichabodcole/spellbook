  unchanged.

  whitespace stripped: they match. `NOT_CONNECTED_REASON` moved to `TopBar.tsx`
  and `WorkspaceShell.tsx` re-exports it, so the shell barrel's `export *` and
  `WorkspaceShell.end.test.tsx` still see it. `CONNECTION_LABEL`,
  `CONNECTION_TITLE` and `PRESENCE` went to `StatusBar.tsx`.
- **The override** is 1,755 (was 2,029), the exact count: 1,754 fails.
