# Compare Atlas and Scriptorium side by side

Step 1 of [Workspace Kit](../features/workspace-kit/feature.md)'s first cycle.
Run Atlas (Story Studio) and Scriptorium side by side and compare the header,
sidebars, status strip and chat. Cole finds Atlas tighter and cleaner in places,
and Atlas tells a connected session apart from an agent that is listening. The
comparison is a look, not a redesign. It informs the theme and primitives this
cycle, and what the ported shell keeps from each next cycle.

**Spellbook keeps its own colour theme** (Cole, 2026-10-13). The comparison
looks at layout, density and behaviour, not at Story Studio's palette.

## Definition of done

- [ ] A write-up with screenshots of both apps, area by area, each with a call:
      keep Atlas's, keep Scriptorium's, or take both
- [ ] The calls that bear on `theme` and `ui` are folded into
      `kit-port-theme-ui` before that item starts
- [ ] Cole has ruled on the calls for the shell, recorded for the next cycle
