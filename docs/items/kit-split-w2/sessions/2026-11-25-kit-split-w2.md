# WorkspaceShell's column and pane-key hooks (W2) — 2026-11-25

Part of [Split the kit](../../../cycles/2026-12-kit-split.md), step W2 of
[feature/kit-split](../../../features/kit-split/feature.md).

## Context

The second step in lane B, after W1. `WorkspaceShell.tsx` was 1,945 lines
(Biome's count 1,755). Two of `ShellBody`'s sections, the column layout and the
pane keys, become custom hooks.

## What Happened

- **Two hooks.** `useShellColumns.ts` (264 lines) takes the column-layout
  section: storage, collapse and expand, double-click on a handle, quiet and
  fade, the chat-unseen mark. `usePaneKeys.ts` (117) takes the pane keys:
  Ctrl+1/2/3, Escape, Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+], and the composer focus. Each call
  replaces its section in place, under the section's own comment, and its body
  is the section's text byte for byte, so hook and effect order is unchanged.
  The composer section between them did not move.
- **What they take and give.**
  `useShellColumns({ storageProp, storageKey, state })` returns the 18 names
  `ShellBody` still uses after the section; its internals stay inside.
  `usePaneKeys({ collapsed, collapse, expand, toggleFrom, floating, floatClosed })`
  returns `focusPane`, `showChatTab`, `focusComposer` and `rootRef`. Every value
  keeps its identity: the refs, setters, `useCallback` and `useMemo` results are
  the original bindings.
- **Constants.** `PANES` and `HANDLE_REACH`, used only by the column section,
  moved with it; `WorkspaceShell.tsx` re-exports `PANES` for the shell barrel,
  as W1 did for `NOT_CONNECTED_REASON`.
- **One dependency added.** `setQuiet` now comes from a hook, so Biome's
  exhaustive-dependencies rule asked for it in the `workspace` memo's list. It
  is the raw `useState` setter, stable across renders, so the memo recomputes no
  more often; the "same object across renders" test in `panes.test.tsx` holds.
- **The override** is 1,498 (was 1,755), the exact count. No test changed. The
  gate's build rebuilt the scriptorium bundle (`chore(dist)`).
