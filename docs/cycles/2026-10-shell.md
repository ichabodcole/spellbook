# The shell

## Why now

The [document pane](2026-10-document-pane.md) landed on 2026-10-18, so the
flagged Scriptorium now reads, edits and notes documents on the kit. It still
lives in the interim frame, which is fixed-width, has no End session, search,
undo, Trash or floating composer, and was always meant to be thrown away. The
[port-shape write-up](../items/workspace-stack-port-shape/write-up.md#2b-the-cycles)
put the shell and the cutover together as cycle 5. A survey on 2026-10-19 found
it too large for one cycle: the shell is about 3.75k lines plus 5.5k of tests
and needs three slots of our own, and the cutover deletes about 20k lines and
recalibrates about 15 wards. So this cycle is the shell, with the flag still
off, and the cutover follows as its own cycle.

**The port base, read on 2026-10-19** (`git show` and `git log` only; Story
Studio's `develop` is at `7832083`):

- **Story Studio's paradigm is diverging from ours** (Cole, 2026-10-19). Its
  idea of a project and of the file tree is changing. So each shell commit since
  `027d7a3` was judged against Scriptorium's model: files edited where they
  live, sets added from anywhere, no home workspace.
- **Wanted:** the floating composer's shell half, a note with the agent, the
  chosen note's passage, via and refs, and the dock above the status strip.
- **Left out:** copy-in, the plugin host, and four commits of the diverging
  paradigm: the blocking workspace prompt, the workspace heading with its
  pencil, Atlas stage drops, and entering a folder as the tree's root.
  [kit-port-shell](../items/kit-port-shell.md) lists each with its reason.
- **The base is pinned at `7832083`** and cut back from there, because picking
  forward from `027d7a3` doesn't apply. Story Studio changed its shell three
  times on 2026-10-19 alone.

## Rulings

1. **`.mdx` documents count, at the basic level (Cole, 2026-10-19, in
   Scriptorium).** They appear in the tree, `meta` and `find`, and are read and
   edited as text, rendered as markdown. MDX's imports and components are not
   run. [kit-mdx-documents](../items/kit-mdx-documents.md) joins. Not taken:
   accepting the loss at the cutover, or running MDX's components.

Cole took the recommendation on each of the rest (2026-10-19, in Scriptorium).

2. **Two cycles.** This cycle is the shell, and the cutover follows as its own
   cycle, so the flipped build gets used for real before anything is deleted.
   Not taken: one cycle with two landings.
3. **The broken-links sign is one mark for the whole session,** as Story
   Studio's link check has it, with its list grouped by set and our "leaving the
   set" count added. Each entry opens the link at its line. Not taken: a mark on
   each set.
4. **End session keeps the shipped warning about unsaved documents,** offered
   back to Story Studio. Not taken: the kit's End session as it is.
5. **Pane sizes reset once at the cutover,** because the kit's layout key
   differs. Everything else in prefs carries over. Not taken: migrating the
   layout key.
6. **For the cutover cycle:** the shipped Scriptorium stays reachable through an
   environment variable between the flip and the deletion, refused once a
   session has converted. Not taken: a flip with no way back but git.

**A later ruling (Cole, 2026-10-20, on the Atlas comparison):**

7. **Every call in the
   [Atlas comparison](../items/kit-atlas-scriptorium-comparison/write-up.md)
   stands as recommended.** The two that keep shipped behaviour the port must
   not lose join this cycle before it lands:
   [kit-composer-closed-waiting-mark](../items/kit-composer-closed-waiting-mark.md)
   (E53's mark with the composer closed) and
   [kit-stalled-note-ask-act](../items/kit-stalled-note-ask-act.md) (E65's act).
   The split divider, the waiting marks after End session and the smaller
   follow-ups are filed for triage.
