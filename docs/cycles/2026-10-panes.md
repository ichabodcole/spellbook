# Chat and context panes

## Why now

The [wiki layer](_archive/2026-10-wiki-layer.md) landed on 2026-10-17, so the
flagged Scriptorium's daemon and CLI now cover everything the shipped one does.
What it still lacks is anything the human can see. The
[port-shape write-up](../items/workspace-stack-port-shape/write-up.md#2b-the-cycles)
puts the chat and context panes third. Two things decided in the last cycle were
explicitly left for here: the move plan's confirm dialog, and asking for a
location when nothing is selected.

**The port base moved since the plan was written** (read with `git show` and
`git log` only, 2026-10-17):

- **Chat** comes from Story Studio's `develop` as it stands. All four commits
  since `027d7a3` are ones we want: the floating composer (`5db9315`), notes
  with the agent (`85a319e`), a chosen note marking its passage (`ae08f10`) and
  via/refs (`bbc54fd`).
- **Context** comes from `027d7a3` plus six commits, leaving out the three that
  copy files into a single project workspace. That copy-in model is Story
  Studio's, not ours.
- **The plugin host touches neither pane.**
- **Size:** about 2.5k + 5.2k lines, against the plan's 1.9k + 4.7k.

## Scope

Run the two ports first and in parallel. The slot and chip items build on them,
and the Scriptorium items come last, on top of everything.

- **[item/kit-port-chat](../items/kit-port-chat.md)**: Story Studio's chat at
  `develop`, with `lib/oneLine.ts`.
- **[item/kit-port-context](../items/kit-port-context.md)**: context from
  `027d7a3` plus six commits, without copy-in.
- **[item/message-via-and-refs](../items/message-via-and-refs.md)**: closes when
  the two ports land the chat half (the composer's `via`, ref chips, the tree
  multi-select).
- **[item/kit-pre-move-confirm-slot](../items/kit-pre-move-confirm-slot.md)**: a
  move can wait for the human's confirm; Scriptorium's `move.plan` fills it.
- **[item/kit-chat-about-chip](../items/kit-chat-about-chip.md)**: the chip
  keeps an app's `about` parts; `entryIds` gets a gesture or is removed.
- **[item/scriptorium-kit-interim-frame](../items/scriptorium-kit-interim-frame.md)**:
  the flagged Scriptorium's first surface, a thin frame around the panes.
- **[item/scriptorium-kit-ask-location](../items/scriptorium-kit-ask-location.md)**:
  ask where to create when nothing is selected.
- Candidates, check when the frame is up:
  [item/kit-docmeta-push-cost](../items/kit-docmeta-push-cost.md) (the surface
  is the first consumer of every push) and
  [item/set-make-relink-offer](../items/set-make-relink-offer.md) (the offer
  needs a pane).

Out of scope, deliberately:

- **The document pane and the map.** They are cycle 4, with `0ff5698`'s
  `DocumentPane` half.
- **The shell, its storage adapter and the floating composer's shell half.**
  These are cycle 5. Story Studio's `kit-floating-composer` has landed
  (`5db9315`), so the port-shape write-up's plan to port our own composer no
  longer applies. Only the shell half remains.
- **Story Studio's copy-in commits** (`6b3d943`, `7afe5d9`, `83ea109`) and
  **their plugin host**: Cole wants their plugin-oriented rework watched for
  ideas that fit, not implemented yet.
- **Hardening before the cutover** (the discovery pointer, concurrent restore,
  symlinked sets, write races) and **session cleanup**: these are not pane work.
- **The shipped Scriptorium.** It stays as it is until the cutover.
