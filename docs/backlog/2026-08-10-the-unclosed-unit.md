## What this kills — state it in the issue, because both are what a maintainer reaches for first

1. **A reminder / checklist line / prompt cannot help.** Every one of the five
   instances already had the reminder, and in two of them the person writing the
   reminder was the person omitting the artifact. An intervention aimed at
   awareness cannot fix a failure whose defining feature is full awareness.
2. **A disjunctive gate cannot help.** A checklist item reading _"outcome doc
   written, **or** the deviation recorded"_ passes **5 of 5**. The escape hatch
   is the failure mode, not a safety valve on it.

So the ask is not "add a close step." It is: **the close step must be gated, and
the gate must not be satisfiable by an acknowledgement.**

## Why it belongs upstream rather than here

The shape is not spellbook's. It reproduced in a second repo, on a different
scaffold version, with different rituals, on the same day, found independently.
Both humans also described the underlying gap unprompted within hours of each
other, neither having read the other.

## The methodological note worth keeping

From the same exchange, and it is the reason this file exists rather than a chat
log:

- **A peer at a different vantage finds what you cannot see; only you can
  falsify what you already believe.** Each side found a defect the other was
  looking straight at and missed — position, not care. But the fifth and
  sharpest finding only surfaced because a claim I had already published, and
  which had already been agreed to, got re-checked and turned out false. Nothing
  forced that, and the channel could not have supplied it.
- **Leaving a finding in a chat channel is itself an instance of this finding**
  — a record in a place nobody re-reads, standing in for the artifact.
